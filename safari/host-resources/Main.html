KnowThankYew Reality Engine for Safari

Local consumer contract analysis for checkout flows, subscription renewals, and arbitration clauses. Runs on your device with no network client entitlement and no external servers.

1. Enable in Safari

  1. Click Open Safari Extension Settings… (or open Safari → Settings → Extensions on macOS / Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions on iOS).
  2. Enable KnowThankYew Reality Engine.
  3. Allow website access when clicking the toolbar icon on checkout pages or grant site access in Safari extension settings.
  4. Open any checkout or Terms of Service page and click the KnowThankYew toolbar button to inspect visible clauses locally.
