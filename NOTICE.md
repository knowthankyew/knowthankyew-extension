## 1. Statutory Educational Tool Notice (Not Legal Advice)
The KnowThankYew Reality Engine is an automated analysis tool highlighting deceptive subscription mechanisms, arbitration provisions, and statutory rights under FTC regulations and state consumer protection statutes. It does not provide legal advice or formal representation.

## 2. Consumer Privacy Defaults & Air-Gap Invariant
All text segmentation, pattern analysis, and risk evaluations execute 100% locally in client-side memory on the user's device. No browsing history, page contents, or form entries are ever transmitted off-box.
